Common questions

What counts as a managed device?

Any network device we scan and monitor: routers, switches, firewalls, access points and load balancers, plus Linux and Windows hosts you choose to benchmark. Printers, phones and IoT endpoints we discover but don't manage do not count.

What happens if I go over my device allowance?

Devices beyond the allowance are billed at $10 per device per month on your next invoice, up to each plan's ceiling: 50 on Starter, 75 to 150 on Team, up to 500 on Pro. Past the ceiling you move to the next plan, which is cheaper at that point anyway. We tell you before any overage bills and nothing stops scanning.

Are any frameworks extra?

No. All 27 frameworks are included on every plan, and you can enable as many as you're held to. Compliance-automation tools commonly charge around $5,000 a year per additional framework; we don't.

What's included in onboarding?

Collector install, device discovery and inventory, credential profiles in your Key Vault, framework scoping and a kickoff review. Most environments are onboarded within two weeks. Starter is self-serve with documentation and no onboarding fee.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect at the next billing cycle and there's no additional onboarding fee; you pay the difference in onboarding only if you move from Starter to a plan that includes it.

I'm an MSP — how does the per-tenant model work?

You pay the $1,500 platform fee plus a wholesale price per client tenant based on its size, listed above. Volume breaks apply from your 10th and 25th tenants. You set the price your clients pay and keep the margin; there is no revenue share and nothing to report. From two clients onward it costs less than buying the direct plans.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed upfront for 12 months at a discount: 10% off Starter and Team, 15% off Pro, Enterprise and the MSP platform fee. Onboarding fees apply once regardless of billing term.

We're preparing for a SOC 2 Type II audit — which plan?

Team or above. Type II needs evidence that controls operated over the audit period, not just on scan day; Team includes the period evidence report, the exceptions register and read-only examiner links your auditor will ask for. Pro adds remediation and integrations if you also want to fix what the audit finds.

Do you offer project work outside of plans?

Yes, for scoped engagements such as one-time audits, network redesigns, segmentation projects or automation builds. Describe the project through the contact form and we'll respond within one business day.
